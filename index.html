CT Audience Manager — Privacy Policy

Updated: October 12, 2026.

1. About this project

CT Audience Manager is a free personal project based in Ukraine. It helps you organise your X audience and optionally publish your own profile in a discovery directory. For privacy, support or deletion requests, email UkrainianVapeMasters@gmail.com. Do not send passwords or session tokens.

2. Data in your browser

The extension processes X profiles, follower/following relationships, available scores, account activity, notes, favourites, snapshots, settings and collection progress. These records are stored locally in your browser. Your full audience list, notes and favourites are not uploaded to our discovery server. Exported files are created at your request; you control who receives them.

To collect X data, the extension uses your existing X session, reads the X CSRF cookie and observes selected X request URLs. It does not collect passwords you type or your general browsing history. X session cookies are not sent to our discovery server or metric providers.

3. External services

Collection uses different methods depending on the source. Moni metrics are requested from its API using public account identifiers. Kaito and Sorsa metrics can be read from supported browser pages, including values displayed by separately installed extensions. Those extensions have their own privacy practices.

Our server can refresh published profiles through Moni, Kaito Voices and FxEmbed. Avatar images load from their source hosts. External services receive the requested identifiers and normal connection information, such as the requesting IP address; for server requests this is the server's address. These requests do not upload your entire audience to our discovery server.

Provider information: X, Moni, Kaito, Sorsa, and FxEmbed documentation. Provider terms apply separately; this policy grants no additional rights to their services or data.

4. Optional profile publication

You can use the local tools without publishing a profile. If you consent and press the publication button, we store your display name, X handle, avatar link, available own-profile metrics and their acquisition dates. Metrics may include Moni Score, Sorsa Score, Kaito Smarts, Moni Smarts and follower, following and post counts. Other directory participants can view your profile and use its metrics for discovery matching and filtering.

We also store your publication choice and technical records needed for access, profile updates and participant connection events. Published profiles can update while your browser is closed. You choose whether to download or share a profile card; posting on X requires your confirmation. Other people may keep or reshare information you publish.

We do not operate a shared database of metrics collected about your entire audience. Profile publication consent does not cover that use.

5. Use and hosting

We use local data for the audience tools and published profile data for the discovery features you request. Optional publication is based on your consent. Access, security and support records are used to operate the service and handle requests, subject to applicable law.

Supabase hosts our directory and authentication services. The database is configured in eu-west-1, but hosting and external services can process information outside Ukraine and outside that region. See Supabase's processing information.

Our use of user data is limited to the disclosed features and their necessary operation, consistent with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including Limited Use requirements. We do not sell user data, use it for personalised advertising or use scores for credit or lending decisions.

6. Storage and deletion

Local records remain until you clear them through the extension or browser, or uninstalling removes them. Delete exported files separately.

Hiding your profile stops directory visibility and scheduled updates but retains its stored records. Removing it in the updated extension deletes your profile, linked directory records and installation authentication account, and clears the saved directory credentials and cache. Your local audience remains. Older versions may leave the installation account; contact us for help or if you lose access.

Profile-linked operational logs currently have no automatic expiry and remain until profile deletion or manual removal. Provider-managed logs and backups, legally required records and copies held by others may remain separately. Contact us about remaining records.

7. Your choices and rights

You can decline publication, hide or remove your profile and clear local records. Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, portability, restriction or objection, withdraw consent or complain to a competent authority. Withdrawal does not affect earlier lawful processing. We handle requests within applicable deadlines and may ask for reasonable evidence that you control the relevant data, never your password.

8. Security and changes

We use HTTPS and backend access controls; no system is completely secure. Discovery is intended for adults. Contact us about unintended data or security issues. We will communicate material changes through the extension or this page and request new consent where required.

CT Audience Manager — Terms of Use

Use the extension for its intended functions and follow applicable law. Do not impersonate people, publish a profile you do not control, submit knowingly false information, harass participants, send spam or interfere with the service. Discovery is intended for adults.

Publishing requires your separate consent and a publication action. Other participants can view your own profile and its available metrics. Your full audience is not uploaded to a shared metrics database.

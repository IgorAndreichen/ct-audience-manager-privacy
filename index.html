CT Audience Manager Privacy Policy

Updated: October 11, 2026

CT Audience Manager provides X audience analytics and voluntary discovery of new connections. Contact the project creator at https://x.com/UkrainianVapeM1 for privacy questions or deletion assistance.

Information processed on your device

The extension stores collected audience profiles, public account metrics, activity observations, relationship information, notes, priorities, snapshots, settings and collection progress in Chrome local storage. Local records are not uploaded as an audience list to our discovery backend. Export and backup files remain under your control.

The extension uses your existing X session for read-only X requests, including reading the X CSRF cookie. It observes selected X request URLs to identify current profile and timeline operations. X authentication cookies are not sent to our backend or metric providers. The extension does not collect general browsing history or passwords entered into websites.

External services

X receives requests needed to retrieve profile, audience and activity data. Moni receives public profile identifiers used to request available metrics. Kaito metrics are read through supported collection paths. Providers process requests under their own policies and may receive ordinary connection metadata such as IP addresses. Avatar images are loaded from their original hosts.

Voluntary discovery

When you choose to publish a discovery profile, the Supabase-hosted project backend stores your public name, X handle, avatar URL, visibility consent and version, and available profile metric observations with retrieval dates. The backend also maintains installation authentication, participant connection events and collection records. Visible profile information is accessible to other directory participants. Discovery events can be visible to the participants involved.

For visible profiles, the backend may retrieve public metrics from Moni, FxEmbed and Kaito Voices without your browser being open. These requests use public identifiers, not your X session. Third-party data may be missing or cached. Retrieved metrics do not verify ownership of an X profile.

Purpose and sharing

We use this information to provide audience analytics, user-selected filters, priorities, discovery, metric refreshes and user-requested sharing. We do not sell personal data or use it for advertising. We do not send local notes, full audience lists or X cookies to the discovery backend. A card and post draft are shared with X only when you choose the sharing action; you confirm publication yourself.

Retention and controls

Local records remain until cleared through the extension, browser storage controls or uninstalling the extension. Exported files must be deleted separately. Hiding a discovery profile removes it from the visible directory and excludes it from further scheduled collection; it does not delete the stored profile. Use Delete profile from directory to remove the directory record and associated linked discovery records. Installation authentication and operational hosting records can remain separately. For assistance with these records, contact the creator above. Operational collection logs currently have no automatic retention deadline; do not assume hiding or deleting a directory profile removes provider-side logs or all hosting records.

Security and changes

Service requests use HTTPS. Backend access controls restrict profile changes to the authenticated installation. This does not establish ownership of the claimed X account. No service can guarantee absolute security. We will update this page when data handling changes; additional consent will be requested when required.

CT Audience Manager's use of information complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

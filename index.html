CT Audience Manager — Privacy Policy

Updated: October 12, 2026.

1. Operator and contact

CT Audience Manager is operated as a personal project based in Ukraine. For privacy, deletion and support requests, contact UkrainianVapeMasters@gmail.com. Project contact also available: https://x.com/UkrainianVapeM1. Do not send passwords, session cookies or private keys in a support request.

2. Local audience tools

The extension processes public X account profiles, audience relationships, available account metrics, activity observations, notes, priorities, snapshots, preferences and collection progress in your browser storage. Other accounts may be present in these local records. Your full audience list, private notes and favourites are not uploaded to our discovery backend. Export and backup files are created at your request and remain under your control; anyone you give a file to can read it. Read-only X collection uses your existing browser session and reads the X CSRF cookie. The extension observes selected X request URLs to identify relevant profile/timeline operations. X authentication cookies are not sent to our discovery backend or metric providers. It does not collect passwords entered into websites or general browsing history.

3. Requests to external services

Collection can send public account identifiers to X and metric providers such as Moni. Kaito and Sorsa values may be read from supported browser collection paths, including values displayed by separately installed extensions. Those extensions and providers operate under their own terms and privacy policies. This policy does not authorise access that a provider prohibits. Server refreshes of visible discovery profiles can use Moni, FxEmbed and Kaito Voices. Avatar images are loaded from their source hosts. Services contacted may receive the requested public identifiers and ordinary network information, including IP address and connection metadata. This is distinct from uploading an audience list to our backend. No paid provider access is automatically purchased by CT Audience Manager.

4. Optional discovery directory

Local audience tools do not require you to publish a profile. Selecting the discovery consent and pressing the publication button makes your declared X profile visible to other directory participants. The backend stores the public display name, handle, avatar URL, consent version, visibility, available own-profile metric observations and acquisition dates. Available data may include Moni Score, Sorsa Score, Kaito Smarts, Moni Smarts and own-profile followers/following/posts counts; not every field is available or transmitted through every collection path. The backend also processes installation authentication identifiers, declared connection events, refresh queue records and operational logs. Visible profiles may be refreshed without the browser being open. Missing values are not invented, and a retrieval timestamp is not a guarantee that the provider has just recalculated the value. We do not verify ownership of the claimed X account merely through installation authentication. A declared follow event is not proof of an X follow. The shared pool of metrics collected about all accounts in participants’ audiences is not enabled. Discovery consent does not authorise that future use. Any future materially different sharing feature requires separate information and consent where required.

5. Purposes and legal bases

The hosted service uses optional profile data to publish the directory entry, calculate discovery matching and refresh that entry with the participant's consent. Consent can be withdrawn by hiding the profile. Installation authentication is used to operate access controls for requested directory functions. Necessary security, abuse-prevention and support records are used to operate and protect the service and respond to requests, subject to applicable law. Legal obligations may require specific records to be retained. We do not sell personal data, use it for advertising targeting, or make credit, employment or similar consequential decisions from scores. Local filters and discovery suggestions are informational. Your consent to publish your own profile does not provide consent on behalf of other people in your audience.

6. Recipients and hosting

Supabase hosts discovery database and authentication services; the configured project region is eu-west-1. Other providers and their subprocessors may process connection or support data under their own arrangements. A project region alone does not establish that every processing operation takes place in the EEA. Other participants receive visible directory information and relevant declared events. X receives user-requested sharing actions, subject to the user's confirmation. Publication can be copied, downloaded or reshared by recipients; we cannot guarantee recall of copies already received. Hosting can involve processing outside Ukraine and outside the selected database region. Supabase describes its processing arrangements, subprocessors and transfer mechanisms in its Data Processing Addendum and Data Residency and Transfers FAQ. These provider documents do not establish that every operation takes place in the EEA or that CT Audience Manager has a compliance certification. Provider information: https://supabase.com/legal/customer-resources/data-processing-addendum and https://supabase.com/legal/privacy-resources/data-residency-and-transfers-faq.

7. Retention and deletion

Local records remain until cleared through the extension or browser storage controls, or until uninstalling removes them. Exports must be deleted separately. Hiding a profile stops directory visibility and eligibility for further scheduled refreshes, but retains the stored record. In the updated extension, removing the directory profile deletes the directory record, linked events, metric observations, collection attempts and refresh queue entries, and the anonymous installation authentication account and its linked sessions. The extension clears its saved installation credentials and discovery cache for that owner. Your local audience records remain under your control. Older extension versions delete the directory record without deleting the installation authentication account; contact us for assistance with remaining records. Provider-managed hosting logs and backups can remain separately. Current collection logs have no configured automatic expiry and can remain until their linked directory record is deleted or they are manually removed. Daily collection totals contain aggregate counts rather than individual profile identifiers. You can contact us to request review and deletion of remaining records; legally required retention and provider-managed backups or logs may limit immediate removal. Removal does not erase independent provider logs or copies made by others. Contact the operator for requests involving remaining records or loss of installation access.

8. Your choices and rights

You can decline publication, hide your profile, remove its directory entry and clear local records. Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, object to qualifying processing, withdraw consent or complain to a competent supervisory authority. Withdrawal does not affect prior lawful processing. We may request proportionate evidence of identity/control to protect others' data; never provide a password or session token. Requests are handled within applicable statutory deadlines; rights and exceptions depend on the relevant law.

9. Security, changes and children

HTTPS and backend access controls are used, but absolute security cannot be guaranteed. Installation authentication does not prove X account ownership. The discovery directory is intended for adults; do not publish a profile for a child or someone else. If unintended data or a security issue is found, contact the operator. Material changes will be communicated through the extension or policy page as appropriate; new consent will be requested where required, not inferred from a historical consent.
